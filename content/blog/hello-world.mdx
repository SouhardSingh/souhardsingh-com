Welcome to my corner of the internet.

I've been meaning to have a proper home online for years. Every time I started building one I'd get distracted by the tooling and never finish. This time I made a rule: ship it first, polish it later.

## What this place is

This site is a few things:

- A **blog** — longer-form thinking on software, ideas, and occasionally travel
- A **digital garden** — half-formed notes and ideas that grow over time
- A **projects** page — the things I've built and what I learned
- A **bookshelf** — what I'm reading and watching

## Why I built it

Mostly because I want a place to think out loud. Writing forces a kind of clarity that thinking alone doesn't. And having a public log of ideas — even half-baked ones — creates useful accountability.

Also, honestly: I wanted an excuse to build with Next.js 16 and Tailwind 4.

More soon.
