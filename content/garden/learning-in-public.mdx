The best thing about writing publicly is that it makes you commit to ideas.

Private notes are free to stay vague forever. Public notes have to be coherent enough for someone else to follow.

This creates useful pressure. Not to be right — you'll be wrong sometimes — but to be clear.

The fear of being wrong in public keeps a lot of people from sharing ideas at all. That's a shame. The cost of a wrong idea shared is low. The cost of never sharing is that your thinking never gets stress-tested.
