A digital garden is different from a blog.

A blog is a stream — posts flow out in chronological order and largely stay as they were on the day they were published. A garden is a space — notes exist in relation to each other, and they evolve.

The metaphor comes from the idea that knowledge grows. A seed of an idea is planted, tended over time, and either blooms into something fully-formed or gets composted back into the soil.

## Stages

I use three stages to track where a note is:

- **Seed** — a raw idea, barely formed
- **Growing** — I've thought about it more, but it's not done
- **Evergreen** — stable enough that I'd send someone a link to it

Most notes live in the "growing" stage for a long time. That's fine.

## Wiki links

Notes here can link to each other using `[[slug]]` syntax. This creates a backlink graph — you can see which notes reference each other. See also [[learning-in-public]].

The goal isn't a perfectly curated knowledge base. It's a live record of how I think.
